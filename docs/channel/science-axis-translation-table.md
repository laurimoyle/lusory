## Why this file exists

The map's "Decisions this surfaces" asks *"whether the science tag axis adopts these family
names verbatim, since they will be visible in the toggle and coaches will read them as
claims."* WP6 answers no. Metaphor labels keep the research recognisable to a coach who
knows it while promising nothing to a player who doesn't.

## How to read the columns

**Basis** — `[title]` near-verbatim from a paper/book title · `[term]` a phrase the
literature itself uses · `[mechanism]` the mechanism in plain language. Weakest is
`[mechanism]`; a `[mechanism]` label is admissible but is an invention, not a citation.

**Grade** — the map's own grade for that anchor, carried verbatim. Rule 4 below requires
every channel use to pair metaphor + family + grade; v1 of this file omitted the grade
column entirely, which is the defect this version exists to fix. *The D grades are the
credibility of the A grades.*

**†** — citation verified in the literature but **not present in the map**. Architect-supplied
addition, not map-derived. Kept where it is the better citation, marked so the provenance
is never mistaken.

## Standing flags

1. **Three labels are inventions, not citations** — `New Ground`, `Small Kindnesses`,
   `Taking the Picture` are all `[mechanism]`: plain-language phrases the literature does
   not itself use. Defensible under the naming principle, but if a coach-facing review
   finds a better in-literature phrase, prefer it. (v1 of this file said "the weakest two";
   it undercounted by one.)
2. **`Fast Friends` is the only shipped label that names an outcome rather than an
   activity** — and the map's caveat is pointed: *"Aron measured closeness, not wellbeing
   per se; and remember his own Study 3 — telling pairs to* aim *at closeness added
   nothing."* That collides with Lusory Conversion Rule 1, the byproduct principle. The
   label is the strongest `[term]` available and the architect has endorsed it; this flag
   exists so the tension is visible rather than resolved by silence.
3. **`Small Kindnesses` sits on a split record.** Any channel use must carry both
   sentences, **in this order**: the cumulative meta-analysis (Curry 2018, δ = 0.28),
   then the preregistered shortfall (Folk & Dunn 2024). The map is explicit about the order.
4. **`The Moving Body` must keep the acute-vs-symptom distinction.** The map: the
   preregistered record for *acute mood* claims is weak; Untracked Miles anchors to
   **symptom-level** umbrella evidence, *"and the claims language must keep that
   distinction."* Never let this label imply a mood lift after a walk.
5. **Active-control shrinkage, two rows.** Gratitude effects *"shrink further against
   active controls"* and it is *"the most famous practice in positive psychology with the
   most thoroughly deflated effect size."* Mindfulness is *"generally no better than other
   active practices"* — which is the argument for a plural library, not a defect.
6. **`One Hour Less` is dose-specific.** It names Brailovskaia's actual intervention.
   Honest while the baseline tier stays near that dose; the Pocket Vacation's harder tier
   (half a day) exceeds it. The label tracks the *family's* evidence, not the tier chosen.
7. **`The Kinder Voice` is B− with an untrialed mechanic** — the map: *"family-level
   attribution; our specific mechanic is untrialed"* and *"this is the one game where the
   character carries mechanics, not just voice — test carefully against the 80/20 rule
   before promoting it."*
8. **`The Ice-Breaker Effect` was withdrawn** in favour of `Singing Together`. It was the
   best-sourced `[term]` in the file — the map quotes it as the paper's own — but it is the
   only candidate label naming an *effect*, and a definite-article "The … Effect" reads as
   an established phenomenon sitting on a B− non-randomised study. Rule 4 forbids exactly
   that reading.
9. **The labels are not claims and must never be used as evidence shorthand.** In channel
   materials always pair metaphor + family + **grade**. The in-app label exists to avoid a
   claim; reusing it bare in a deck would smuggle one back in.

  materially false. Floor Is Lava carries an **A anchor** (Singh 2023) with **D
  specificity**; The 24-Hour Reply is **D** with temporal self-distancing as a stretch.
- **Added the missing `Unkeepable Collecting` (C)** bench row.
- **Added a grade column** to all 23 rows, per rule 4.
- **Restored Smyth 1998** (the map's effect-size citation for expressive writing), the
  **Etkin 2016** half of Untracked Miles' anchor, and the **Koo et al. 2008 C+ sub-anchor**
  inside Three Good Finds. A C+ finding riding inside a B+ frame is precisely what this
  file exists to keep visible.
- **Downgraded two markings.** `Seeking Awkwardness` [title]→[term]: the paper is titled
  *"Motivating Personal Growth by Seeking Discomfort"* — awkwardness was the instruction
  manipulation, not the title. `One Hour Less` [term]→[mechanism]: a dose is not a
  construct name.
- **Marked † provenance** on Piff 2015, Pennebaker 1997 and Emmons & McCullough 2003 —
  all real and well-chosen, none present in the map.
- **Fixed the Green Time row's cell count** (4 cells under a 3-column header in v1), which
  would have silently dropped the association-never-causation caveat in many renderers.
