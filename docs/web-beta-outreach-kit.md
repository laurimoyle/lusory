## Approved public-community post

Title: Test one contemplative real-world game — private web beta

I am recruiting adults for the private web beta of Lusory, a shelf of fifteen contemplative

## LinkedIn founder post

I am opening a small private web beta for Lusory.

Lusory is a shelf of fifteen contemplative games that happen away from the screen. You pick
a goal, accept an unnecessary obstacle, do the thing in ordinary life, and return to one
question. No streaks, scores, player profile, or individual behavioral analytics.

I am initially looking for adult players plus a few therapists, researchers, coaches, and

## Fifteen-minute professional conversation

1. Before opening it, what did you expect Lusory to be?
2. Where did the product explain itself well, and where did it over-explain?
3. Did the three difficulty doors feel equal or ranked?
4. What would make you unwilling to recommend, study, or pilot it?
5. Does the aggregate-only privacy model remove necessary information for your use case?
6. What claim would you require evidence for first?
7. If Lusory disappeared tomorrow, what—if anything—would be worth rebuilding?

Close by thanking the participant. Do not ask them to recruit others, provide a testimonial,
or join the Android test during the interview. Those invitations can be made separately to
the consented beta email cohort after the web round is reviewed.
